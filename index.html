Frame

A personal photo slideshow and screensaver for Google TV.

Frame shows photos from Google Photos shared albums on your TV, with the time and the weather. It is a private, non-commercial app made for one household.

Optional: Frame can back up its own settings (album links, slideshow settings, hidden photos) to a hidden app folder in your Google Drive, so they come back after a reinstall.
