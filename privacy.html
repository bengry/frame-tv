Frame privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

What Frame stores

Frame stores its settings on your TV: the Google Photos shared-album links you add, slideshow settings, the list of photos you chose to hide, and your weather city.

Google Drive backup and Add from phone (optional, off by default)

If you link a Google account for backup or "Add from phone", Frame asks for drive.file (access only to files and folders Frame creates) and email (to show which account is signed in on the TV). Frame stores its files in a folder named Frame TV app data in your Google Drive, containing only files Frame created; Frame can't see your other files.

When you use Add albums from your phone (add.html), the page signs in with drive.file directly in your browser and saves your pasted album links to pending-albums.json inside that Frame TV app data folder so your TV can pick them up.

What Frame does not do

Frame has no servers. It does not collect, sell or share personal data, and it has no ads or analytics. Your data goes only between your TV (or your browser on add.html) and Google's services (Google Photos, Google Drive), public weather services (Open-Meteo forecast and geocoding), which receive only the city or coordinates for the forecast, and, when no city is set, an IP-location service (GeoJS or ipwho.is) to find your approximate city.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Frame's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Turn off backup or press "Sign out" in Frame's Settings, delete the Frame TV app data folder in Google Drive if desired, and remove Frame's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling Frame deletes all data on the TV.
