Frame privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

What Frame stores

Frame stores its settings on your TV: the Google Photos shared-album links you add, slideshow settings, the list of photos you chose to hide, and your weather city.

Google Drive backup (optional, off by default)

If you turn on backup, Frame asks for one permission: drive.appdata ("see, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive"). Frame then saves one settings file in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Frame cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

What Frame does not do

Frame has no servers. It does not collect, sell or share personal data, and it has no ads or analytics. Your data goes only between your TV and Google's services (Google Photos, Google Drive) public weather services (Open-Meteo forecast and geocoding), which receive only the city or coordinates for the forecast, and, when no city is set, an IP-location service (GeoJS or ipwho.is) to find your approximate city.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Frame's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Turn off backup or press "Sign out" in Frame's Settings, and remove Frame's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling Frame deletes all data on the TV.
